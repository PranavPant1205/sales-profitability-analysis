# Sales & Profitability Analysis – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview
This end-to-end data analyst project explores regional sales performance, profitability trends, and the impact of discounting on business margins using the Superstore dataset.

The analysis was performed using **SQL for data exploration** and **Power BI for interactive dashboarding and business storytelling**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
Despite strong sales, certain regions show declining profitability.  
This project identifies **loss-making regions, root causes, and strategic actions** to improve margins.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- SQL (data analysis & aggregation)
- Power BI (dashboard & visualization)
- Data Cleaning & KPI Design
- Business Insight Generation

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- The **Central region** has the lowest profit margin and contributes over **$56K in losses**.
- **Office Supplies** is the primary loss-making category.
- Higher **average discounts strongly correlate with negative profitability**.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Reduce excessive discounting in the Central region, especially for Office Supplies.
- Align pricing and discount strategy with high-performing regions such as the West.
- Implement margin-based discount monitoring to prevent future losses.
